Return and Refund Policy

Our products are made to order, and many are personalized. Please review your selections and delivery details before placing an order.

Damaged, defective, or incorrect orders

If an item arrives damaged, defective, or different from what you ordered, email us at hello.creative.souls3d@gmail.com with your order ID, a description of the issue, and clear photos of the item and packaging. We’ll review the details and contact you about an appropriate resolution.

Change-of-mind returns

Because personalized products are made for each order, we generally cannot accept returns or exchanges for a change of mind or for customer-provided personalization details entered incorrectly. This does not affect applicable consumer rights.

Refunds

When a refund is approved, we’ll confirm the amount and payment method with you. Processing time may depend on the payment provider or bank.

For help, contact hello.creative.souls3d@gmail.com.
