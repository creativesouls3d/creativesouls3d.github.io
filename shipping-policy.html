Shipping Policy

We prepare personalized 3D printed products with care. Most orders are dispatched within 1–2 days after the order is confirmed. Custom, large, or unusually detailed orders may need additional preparation time.

Delivery estimates

Delivery time depends on the destination and the carrier. Any delivery estimate shown at checkout or shared in an order update is an estimate and may change due to carrier or weather delays.

Tracking

Use the Track Order page and your order ID to check the latest status. If tracking has not updated or your parcel appears delayed, contact us with your order ID.

Address details

Please check your delivery address and phone number before placing the order. Contact us as soon as possible if you notice an error; address changes may not be possible after dispatch.
