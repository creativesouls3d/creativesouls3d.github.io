Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to dispatch my order?

Most orders are prepared for dispatch within 1–2 days. Custom or unusually detailed pieces may take longer; we’ll share updates if that applies to your order.

How can I track my order?

Use the Track Order link on the website and enter your order ID to see the latest order status.

Can I request a personalized design?

Yes. Email hello.creative.souls3d@gmail.com or DM us on Instagram @creative.souls3d with your idea and reference details.

Which payment options are available?

Available payment options are shown at checkout. Cash on Delivery is available for eligible orders.

What if my order arrives damaged or incorrect?

Please contact us at hello.creative.souls3d@gmail.com with your order ID and photos of the item and packaging so we can review and help.
